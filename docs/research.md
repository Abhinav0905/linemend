## Chosen scope

LineMend addresses a specific handover problem for small fabrication workshops: turn a note about a disruption into visible constraints, calculate an alternate daily schedule and expose promises that cannot be kept. The demonstration is concrete enough to inspect in two minutes.

The difference is a combination of narrow workflow, editable evidence, machine-specific routes, a defined old-queue baseline, explicit impossible-deadline lower bounds and immediate counterfactual repair-time changes. No claim that scheduling optimization, evidence checks or AI planners are new. No claim that this idea will win.

Large physical deployments, model training, ERP integrations, multi-agent orchestration and a general factory digital twin would consume the budget without improving the demonstrated core. They are excluded.

## Delivery boundaries

As of this document, the local app, tests and build work. Account setup, live inference, browser visual QA, public video and Devpost submission remain separate checkpoints. Do not mark them complete based on the deterministic examples or mocked provider tests.
